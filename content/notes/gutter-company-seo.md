Most people only think about their gutters twice a year: when the leaves come down, and when water pours over the edge in a storm. Gutter searches follow that pattern, and the companies that win are the ones whose pages are already ranking when it starts.

## Split the services

"Gutters" covers four different jobs, and people search for each one separately:

1. **Gutter cleaning.** Recurring, seasonal, price-sensitive.
2. **Gutter repair.** Sagging, leaking, pulled-away sections. Often urgent after a storm.
3. **Gutter installation and replacement.** Seamless gutters, materials, colors. A bigger, planned purchase.
4. **Gutter guards.** Their own search, with their own comparison questions.

Each deserves its own page. One "Gutter services" page can't rank for all four.

## What each page should answer

- **Cleaning:** what's included (downspouts flushed? debris hauled?), how often to book, and a starting price or what changes it, such as the number of stories.
- **Repair:** common problems and what fixing them involves, and when repair stops making sense.
- **Installation:** materials, seamless versus sectional, colors, how long it takes, and photos of finished jobs.
- **Guards:** the types you install, honest pros and cons, and who they suit.

Plain answers help these pages show up for the questions people search, such as "how often should gutters be cleaned."

## Publish before the season

Pages need time to get crawled and ranked. Publish or refresh your cleaning page in late summer, before fall searches pick up, and your repair page before storm season. Our [seasonal content calendar](/notes/seasonal-content-calendar-home-services) maps the timing for each trade.

## The Business Profile

- **Primary category** for the work most of your revenue comes from, with secondaries only for work you do. See [categories by trade](/notes/gbp-categories-by-trade).
- **Services listed** one by one: cleaning, repair, installation, guards.
- **Photos** of real jobs, especially before and after cleanings and new installs.
- **Reviews** that mention the service and town. Ask right after a cleaning, when the result is visible.

## Town pages

If you serve several towns, give each a page with jobs done there and anything local, such as heavy tree cover in older neighborhoods. Swapping town names on one template doesn't work; [service area pages](/notes/roofing-service-area-pages) explains how to do it properly.

## Pair with roofers

Many gutter jobs come from roof work, and many roofers don't do gutters. A referral relationship with a local roofer can bring jobs, and a link from their site to yours helps your SEO. Mention partners on your site only with their permission.

## Track the calls

Count calls and form requests in GA4 so you know which pages and seasons bring work. [Call tracking for contractors](/notes/call-tracking-for-contractors) shows how.

## Next step

Check whether you have a separate page for each of the four services. If not, start with whichever matches the next season: cleaning before fall, repair before storms. If your calls also come from roof work, our [roofing SEO notes](/notes/topic/roofing) cover the overlap.
