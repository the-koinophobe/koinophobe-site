Brevard County is about 70 miles of coastline with towns that barely overlap. Someone in Titusville doesn't search the same way as someone in Palm Bay, and Google knows the difference. We've done SEO for roofing, pool deck, shutter, lawn, wellness and real estate sites here, and the pattern is the same each time: the contractors who win treat Brevard as a set of towns, not one market.

## How homeowners search here

Searches split by town and by area:

- **North:** Titusville, Mims, Port St. John
- **Central:** Cocoa, Rockledge, Merritt Island, Cocoa Beach, Cape Canaveral, Viera
- **South:** Melbourne, West Melbourne, Palm Bay, Malabar, the beachside down to Melbourne Beach

People type "[service] [town]" or "[service] near me" from wherever they are. A business in Palm Bay rarely shows up in the map pack for a "near me" search made in Titusville. Distance is one of the main things the map uses.

## Pages first, town by town

The fix for distance is town pages, because the regular results below the map lean more on relevance than on distance. Build a page for each town you want work in, and make each one worth reading:

- Jobs you've done there, with photos
- Neighborhoods or areas you cover
- Anything particular to that town: older homes in Cocoa, newer construction in Viera, salt air on the beachside
- A review from a customer there

Copying one page and swapping the town name doesn't work. We cover the full method in [roofing service area pages](/notes/roofing-service-area-pages), and it applies to every trade.

## The Business Profile

One profile, at your real address, with a service area covering the towns you serve. If you don't have a storefront, hide the address. See [service area businesses on Google](/notes/service-area-business-google-business-profile).

A second profile for a "Melbourne office" that's really a mailbox is the fastest way to a suspension. If it happens, our [suspended profile guide](/notes/google-business-profile-suspended) explains the way back.

## Storm season changes the searches

From June to November, storm searches pile up around roofs, shutters, tree removal, water damage and pool decks. Pages built before the season can rank when the searches spike. Our [hurricane shutter case](/notes/hurricane-shutter-seo-next-county) shows how a shutter company won the county next door by building ahead.
