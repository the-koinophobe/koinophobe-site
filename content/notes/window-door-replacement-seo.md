Nobody replaces windows on impulse. Homeowners read about materials, compare brands, ask about energy savings and get two or three quotes. By the time they call, they've already decided which companies look trustworthy, mostly based on what they found during that research.

## Pages for every stage of the research

**Early research:**
- Signs it's time to replace windows
- Vinyl vs wood vs fiberglass vs aluminum
- Window styles: double-hung, casement, sliding, picture
- How much replacement windows cost, with honest ranges and what moves them

**Comparing:**
- The brands and product lines you install, and why you chose them
- Energy efficiency explained: what the ratings mean, without promising specific savings
- Impact windows and doors, if you're in hurricane country

**Deciding:**
- Your installation process, step by step
- Warranties, both product and labor
- Financing, if you offer it

Each of these answers a search people make before they're ready to call. Together they make your site the one homeowners keep coming back to.

## Doors too

Entry doors, patio and sliding doors, and storm doors are searched separately. Give each its own page if you install it.

## Real installs, captioned

A gallery of installs in your towns, each with the town, the product, the style and what changed for the homeowner. Photos inside and outside the home help. Our [before and after gallery guide](/notes/before-after-gallery-seo) explains how to make them help SEO.

## In Florida: impact products

Impact windows and doors bring their own searches: hurricane protection, insurance discounts, code requirements. Explain the general picture and point homeowners to their insurer and the local building department for specifics. If you also install shutters, our [hurricane shutter case](/notes/hurricane-shutter-seo-next-county) shows how a company won the next county over.

## Business Profile

- **Primary category** matching your main work, such as a window installation or window supplier category, with door-related categories only if you do that work
- **Services** listed by product
- **Photos** from real installs, added monthly
- **Reviews** that mention the product, the crew and the town

## Town pages

Replacement window buyers search by town. A page for each town you want work in, with installs from there, beats one regional page. See [service area pages](/notes/roofing-service-area-pages).

## Track consultations, not just clicks

The lead here is a booked consultation. Count consultation requests and calls in GA4 by page, so you know which research pages lead to appointments. [Form tracking in GA4](/notes/ga4-form-tracking-contractors) covers it.
