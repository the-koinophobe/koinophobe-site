"Deep cleaning near me" is a search people make right before they book: moving, hosting, selling, or catching up after a hard few months. They're ready to pay. The page that answers their questions fastest gets the booking.

Most cleaning sites mention deep cleaning in one line on a services page. Here's what a page of its own should have.

## Start with what's included

People searching for deep cleaning want to know what they'll get. Lay it out by room:

- **Kitchen:** inside the microwave, oven exterior (and inside, if offered), cabinet fronts, backsplash, sink and fixtures, under small appliances
- **Bathrooms:** grout scrubbing, shower glass and tracks, toilet base and behind, vanity drawers wiped
- **Living areas:** baseboards, door frames, light switches, blinds, ceiling fans, under and behind movable furniture
- **Bedrooms:** the same detail, plus closets if included

Then say what isn't included, such as carpet shampooing, inside windows or heavy mold. Clear limits prevent bad reviews later.

## Answer the questions people actually ask

Every deep cleaning call has the same few questions. Put the answers on the page:

- **How long does it take?** A range by home size, and whether one cleaner or a team comes.
- **How much does it cost?** A starting price or a range. If you can't publish one, list what changes it: square footage, bathrooms, pets, condition, add-ons.
- **What do I need to do first?** Pick up clutter, secure pets, note any problem areas.
- **Do you bring supplies?** And whether you can use theirs if they prefer certain products.
- **What if something's missed?** Your re-clean policy, in one sentence.

These answers also help the page show up for longer searches like "how long does a deep clean take."

## Make it local

Add a short section on the towns you serve and the kind of homes you clean there. If you have reviews mentioning deep cleans in a specific town, quote one. That gives Google a reason to show the page for local searches, not just the general term.

If you serve several towns, link to your town pages rather than copying this page for each.

## Show the work

Before and after photos of real deep cleans, with permission, do more than any paragraph. Caption each one with the room and what was done. Our [before and after gallery guide](/notes/before-after-gallery-seo) explains how to make photos help SEO too.

## One clear way to book

Put a booking button or a short form near the top and again at the bottom. Ask only what you need to quote: home size, bathrooms, preferred date, anything unusual. Long forms lose bookings.

Track the form and the phone number so you know the page is working. The [three events to track](/notes/three-events-local-business) shows how.

## Title and description that earn the click

Use the search people make: "Deep Cleaning Service in [Town] | [Business Name]," with a description that mentions what's included and a starting price if you have one.
