Pool service is one of the best recurring businesses in home services: a customer who signs up for weekly cleaning can stay for years. It also has an urgent side, when a pump fails or the water turns green the week before a party. Your site should win both.

If you work on pool decks, our [pool deck repair note](/notes/pool-deck-repair-seo) covers that side. This one is about service and repair.

## Separate the recurring from the urgent

**Weekly or biweekly service** is chosen on price, reliability and trust. People search "pool cleaning service near me" and compare a few companies.

**Repairs** are searched by symptom: "pool pump not priming," "pool heater not working," "green pool cleanup." The person wants it fixed soon.

Build pages for both:

- Weekly pool service: what's included (chemicals, skimming, brushing, filter checks), frequency, pricing
- Green pool cleanup
- Pump and motor repair
- Filter cleaning and repair
- Heater repair
- Equipment installation and upgrades
- Pool opening and closing, in seasonal climates

## Pricing for weekly service

Publish a starting monthly price, or what changes it: pool size, screen enclosure, chemicals included or not, frequency. It's the first thing people compare.

## Route-based neighborhood pages

Pool service is a route business. Pages for the neighborhoods and towns where you already service pools, and want more, help you add stops on routes you already drive. Mention typical pool setups in the area, such as screened enclosures, saltwater systems or older plaster. See [service area pages](/notes/roofing-service-area-pages) for how to make each page worth indexing.

## Business Profile

- **Primary category:** a pool cleaning or pool repair category, whichever brings most of your revenue. See [categories by trade](/notes/gbp-categories-by-trade).
- **Services** listed one by one
- **Photos:** clean pools, equipment you've installed, your trucks
- **Reviews:** ask after the first month of weekly service, and right after a green pool turnaround, which customers love to talk about. Our [review script](/notes/how-to-ask-for-google-reviews) has wording.

## Answer the questions customers ask

- How often should a pool be cleaned?
- Why is my pool green?
- How much does weekly pool service cost?
- Do I need to be home?

Short pages or sections for each help you show up for these searches and save phone time.

## Time it to the season

Publish and refresh pages before the season starts, and before peak moving months, when new homeowners look for service. Our [seasonal content calendar](/notes/seasonal-content-calendar-home-services) has the timing by trade.

## Track sign-ups and repair calls separately

Count weekly service sign-ups and repair calls as separate things in your [lead sheet](/notes/lead-tracking-sheet). They're worth very different amounts, and you'll want to know which pages bring which.

## Next step

Check whether you have a separate weekly service page and at least three repair pages. If not, start with weekly service, before your spring rush.
