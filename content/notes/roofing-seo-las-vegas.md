Our Search Console shows a few searches for roofing SEO in Las Vegas. Most of our own roofing work is in Florida, so this note is honest about that: it's what we'd do for a Vegas roofer, based on the same method and on how the market differs.

## What's different about the desert

Florida roofers write about hurricanes. In Las Vegas, the weather problem is heat and sun:

- **Heat and UV.** Underlayment breaks down under tile, sealants dry out, shingles age faster.
- **Tile everywhere.** Concrete and clay tile are common, and "cracked roof tiles" and "underlayment replacement" are real searches.
- **Flat and low-slope roofs.** Foam and other coatings need regular maintenance and recoating.
- **Monsoon storms.** Summer storms bring sudden leak searches.
- **HOAs and planned communities.** Summerlin, Henderson and other master-planned areas have rules about materials and colors.

The searches follow those problems.

## Pages to build

1. **A page per roof type you work on**: tile repair, tile underlayment replacement, foam roofing, flat roof coating, shingle repair and replacement.
2. **Problem pages in homeowners' words**: "roof leak during monsoon," "cracked roof tiles," "roof coating maintenance."
3. **Area pages** for the parts of the valley you want work in: Henderson, Summerlin, North Las Vegas, Spring Valley, with jobs from each. See [service area pages](/notes/roofing-service-area-pages).
4. **An HOA page** explaining how you handle approvals, without quoting rules that change.
5. **A roof replacement page** with honest cost factors. Our [replacement page guide](/notes/roof-replacement-service-page) covers the structure.

## Business Profile

Roofing contractor as primary category, services listed by roof type, photos of real tile and flat-roof jobs, and reviews that mention the area and the job. The full list is in [Business Profile for roofers](/notes/google-business-profile-for-roofers).

## Time pages to the seasons

Publish monsoon and leak pages before summer, so they're indexed when storms hit. Heat-damage and coating pages do well in spring, when homeowners plan maintenance before the hottest months.

## Watch for lead sites

In big metros, lead-generation sites and directories often take the top regular results. You won't always outrank them for the broadest searches, but you can win the specific ones: a roof type plus an area, or a problem plus an area. Those are also the searches that turn into jobs.
