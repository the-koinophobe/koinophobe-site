For many homeowners, a roof inspection is the first time they deal with a roofer. It's lower stakes than a replacement, and it decides who they call when the roof does need work. A strong inspection page ranks for a set of searches most roofers ignore, and starts relationships that turn into jobs.

## Who's searching

- **Home buyers and their agents**, during a purchase
- **Sellers**, before listing
- **Insurance-driven inspections**, for renewals or after a claim
- **Homeowners after a storm**, worried about damage they can't see
- **Owners of older roofs**, wondering how long they have left

Give each a short section, because their questions differ.

## Say exactly what's included

- What you look at: shingles or tiles, flashing, vents, gutters, decking from the attic if you check it
- Whether you go on the roof or use drones or binoculars
- How long it takes
- What the report includes: photos, condition by area, estimated remaining life, prioritized repairs
- When they'll get it

## Be clear about price and obligation

Free or paid, say so, and say there's no obligation if that's true. Homeowners worry that a free inspection is a sales trap. Saying plainly that you'll tell them when a roof is fine builds the trust that brings them back when it isn't.

## Show a sample report

A page with an example report, with photos and the client's details removed, does more than any description. It shows how thorough you are.

## Make it local

Mention the towns you inspect in, and anything local: tile roofs in some neighborhoods, older shingle roofs in others, storm history. Link to your [town pages](/notes/roofing-service-area-pages).

## Connect it to repairs and replacements

Link from the inspection page to your [repair page](/notes/roof-repair-service-page) and [replacement page](/notes/roof-replacement-service-page), and back. A homeowner whose inspection finds a problem should be one click from your explanation of the fix.

## Storm inspections

After storms, inspection searches spike. Mention storm inspections on this page and link to your [storm damage page](/notes/storm-damage-roofing-pages). If you help with insurance claims, link to the page that explains how you help.

## Business Profile

List roof inspection as a service with a short description. Ask inspection customers for reviews, even when no work followed; "they told us our roof was fine and didn't try to sell us anything" is one of the most convincing reviews a roofer can get. Our [review script](/notes/how-to-ask-for-google-reviews) has the wording.

## Next step

Write down what your inspection actually includes and what the customer receives. If your site doesn't say it clearly on its own page, build that page next.
