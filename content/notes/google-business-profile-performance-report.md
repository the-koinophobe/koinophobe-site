For a lot of home service businesses, the map brings more leads than the website does. Google counts most of them in your Business Profile's performance report, and most owners never open it.

## Where to find it

Search for your business on Google while signed in to the account that manages the profile, then open **Performance** from the management panel. You can also open it from the Business Profile manager.

The report shows the last several months. Pick a date range and Google shows totals and a chart.

## The numbers that matter

**Calls.** Taps on the call button in your profile. For a contractor, this is usually the most valuable number in the report.

**Website clicks.** Taps on the website button. Not every one becomes a visit you can see in GA4, but the trend tells you whether people want to know more.

**Directions.** Requests for driving directions. Useful if customers come to you. For a service business that goes to the customer, it's a minor number.

**Bookings and messages**, if you've turned them on.

**Searches.** The report also lists the searches that showed your profile. Treat these as a guide to what people look for, not exact counts.

## Compare months the right way

Home service demand is seasonal, so compare each month with the same month last year. A drop from July to January may be winter, not a problem with the profile.

When you do see a real drop, check in this order:

1. **Was the profile edited?** A changed category, name or address can cause a dip.
2. **Did a competitor appear?** Search your main service from a few spots in your area.
3. **Did reviews slow down?** Recent reviews help, and a long gap shows.
4. **Is the profile still verified and live?** Suspensions happen. Our [suspended profile guide](/notes/google-business-profile-suspended) covers that case.

## What the report misses

- **Calls dialled by hand.** People who read your number and type it in aren't counted. A [tracking number on the profile](/notes/call-tracking-software-contractors), with your real number as the additional phone, catches them.
- **Whether the call became a job.** Only your own records show that.
- **What happened on the website.** Add UTM tags to your profile's website link (for example `?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp`) so those visits show up separately in GA4.

## Put it in your monthly report

Add profile calls and website clicks to your monthly numbers, next to calls and forms from the website. Our [one-page report template](/notes/monthly-seo-report-template) has a row for each. Seeing them together shows which side of your search presence is doing the work.

## Next step

Open the performance report now and write down last month's calls and the same month last year. If calls are down and you can't tell why, the four checks above will usually find it. For the rest of the profile, start with our [local SEO checklist](/notes/local-seo-checklist-home-services).
