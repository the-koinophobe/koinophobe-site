Homeowners choose a painter mostly by looking: at finished rooms, clean lines and a crew that seems careful. Your website and Business Profile need to show that quickly, and show it for the specific job they're searching for.

## Split the services

Painting searches split clearly, and each deserves its own page:

1. **Interior painting**: rooms, ceilings, trim, whole-house repaints
2. **Exterior painting**: siding, stucco, trim, decks and fences if you do them
3. **Cabinet painting and refinishing**: its own popular search and a different buyer
4. **Commercial painting**, if you do it
5. **Specialty work** you're known for, such as stucco repair or pressure washing before a repaint

Each page should say what's included, how prep is done, how long a typical job takes, and what changes the price.

## The project gallery is your best page

Painting is visual. Build a gallery of real jobs, each with:

- A before and after
- The town and neighborhood
- The surface and the color, by name if the homeowner agrees
- A sentence about the job: "Two-story stucco exterior in Viera, repaired cracks before painting"

These details help you show up for local searches and for searches that include colors or materials. Our [before and after gallery guide](/notes/before-after-gallery-seo) covers the setup.

## Business Profile setup

- **Primary category:** Painter, with secondary categories only for work you do, such as cabinet refinishing or pressure washing service.
- **Services** listed one by one.
- **Photos** added every month from real jobs.
- **Reviews** that mention the crew, the cleanup and the result. Ask after the final walkthrough, when the homeowner is happiest.

## Answer the cost question

"How much does it cost to paint a house" is one of the most common searches in the trade. A page with honest ranges from your own jobs, and the factors that move the price, can rank for it and filter for serious leads. Be clear that a site visit sets the final price.

## Seasons matter

Exterior painting slows in rain or cold. Publish and refresh exterior pages before your busy season, and push interior and cabinet work in the slow months. Our [seasonal content calendar](/notes/seasonal-content-calendar-home-services) has the timing by trade.

## Town pages

For each town you want work in, a page with jobs from there. Exterior painting especially varies by town: older wood siding in one area, stucco in another, HOA color rules in planned communities. [Service area pages](/notes/roofing-service-area-pages) explains how to build them so they don't look like copies.
