Our [Business Profile guide for roofers](/notes/google-business-profile-for-roofers) covers the setup: categories, services, photos, reviews. This is what comes after. A profile that's set up once and left alone slowly loses ground to roofers who keep theirs active. Here's the monthly checklist we run.

## Every month

**1. Check the basics are still right.** Name, address or service area, phone, website link, hours. Google and the public can suggest edits, and sometimes they get accepted without you noticing.

**2. Add new photos.** Five to ten from real jobs this month: before and after, crews on site, finished roofs. Caption them in your head with the town and the job, and use those details in posts.

**3. Reply to every new review.** Thank people by name and mention the job and the town. Reply calmly to any negative review, once, with an offer to fix it offline.

**4. Ask for reviews.** Send your review link to every customer whose job finished this month. Our [review script](/notes/how-to-ask-for-google-reviews) has wording, and [Google reviews for roofers](/notes/google-reviews-for-roofers) covers the roofing specifics.

**5. Post an update.** One or two posts: a recent project, storm inspections in a town, a seasonal reminder. Keep them short and real.

**6. Answer questions.** Check the questions section, if your profile shows one, and answer any new ones.

**7. Read the performance report.** Calls, website clicks and directions, against the same month last year. See [the performance report explained](/notes/google-business-profile-performance-report).

**8. Check the competition.** Search your main service from a couple of spots in your area. Note who's above you and what they're doing that you aren't.

## Every quarter

- Review categories and services. Remove anything you've stopped doing. See [secondary categories](/notes/gbp-secondary-categories).
- Check your service area still matches where you work. See [how many service areas](/notes/gbp-service-area-how-many).
- Look for fake or duplicate listings near you and [report them](/notes/report-fake-google-business-listings).

## Storm season

Check the profile weekly during storm season. Update hours if you extend them, post about storm inspections and tarping, add photos of storm work, and keep up with the extra reviews.

## Keep a change log

Write down every edit with a date. When calls move, the log explains why. If the profile is ever suspended, it helps with the appeal. See [suspended profiles](/notes/google-business-profile-suspended).
