After a storm, "roof insurance claim" searches climb fast. Homeowners are stressed and unsure what to do first. A roofer who explains the process clearly earns their trust, and their call.

It's also an area where roofers need to be careful about what they promise.

## What the page should cover

**1. What to do right after damage.** Stay safe, take photos, prevent further damage if you can (a tarp), and contact the insurer. Simple steps, in order.

**2. How a claim usually works.** The homeowner files with their insurer, an adjuster inspects, the insurer decides what's covered, and repairs go ahead. Keep it general, because policies and states differ.

**3. What you do.** Be specific: inspect and photograph the damage, write an estimate, meet the adjuster if the homeowner wants, do the repair or replacement. Only list what you actually do.

**4. What you don't do.** If you don't negotiate with insurers on the homeowner's behalf, say so. In many states that's a role for the homeowner or a licensed public adjuster.

**5. Timelines.** Rough expectations for inspection, adjuster visits and scheduling after a big storm, when everyone is busy.

## What not to say

- **Don't promise coverage.** "We'll get your roof paid for" isn't something a roofer can promise.
- **Don't offer to cover or waive the deductible.** In many places that's not allowed, and it's the kind of offer storm chasers use.
- **Don't imply you're the insurer's partner** unless you're on a formal program.

If you're not sure what your state allows, check before publishing. Clear, careful pages win more trust than bold ones.

## Make it local

Mention the storms and damage types common in your area, and link to your [storm damage page](/notes/storm-damage-roofing-pages) and [roof inspection page](/notes/roof-inspection-page). In hurricane country, our [Florida roofing note](/notes/roofing-seo-florida) covers the wider picture.

## Show real documentation

A sample of how you document damage, such as annotated photos with details removed, shows homeowners what they'll get and helps the page stand out.

## Publish before storm season

A page that already exists and is indexed can rank when storms hit. One written the day after usually can't. Publish and refresh it well before your storm season.
