Move-out cleaning is booked under pressure. A tenant needs the deposit back by Friday. A seller has a closing date. A property manager needs the unit ready for the next lease. They search, compare two or three companies quickly, and book whoever answers their questions clearly.

That makes it one of the best searches a cleaning company can win.

## Who's searching

- **Tenants** moving out who want their deposit back
- **Landlords and property managers** turning over units, often again and again
- **Home sellers** getting a house ready for photos or closing
- **Real estate agents** booking for their clients

One page can serve all four, with a short section for each.

## What the page needs

**1. A checklist of what you clean.** By room: inside cabinets and drawers, inside appliances, baseboards, light fixtures, bathrooms top to bottom, floors. The checklist is the answer to "what does move-out cleaning include," and it's what tenants compare against their lease.

**2. What you don't do.** Carpet shampooing, wall repairs, trash hauling, unless you do. Clear limits prevent disputes.

**3. Price, or what sets it.** A starting price by bedrooms and bathrooms, or the factors: home size, condition, appliances, whether it's furnished.

**4. Scheduling.** How far ahead people should book, whether you do same-week or weekend cleans, and whether the home needs to be empty.

**5. A re-clean promise.** If a landlord's inspection finds something you missed, what happens? One sentence here wins a lot of bookings.

## Make it local

Add the towns you serve and, if it's true, mention the apartment complexes or neighborhoods where you clean most often. Reviews from tenants and landlords in those towns help the page rank and help people trust you. For building town pages properly, see [service area pages](/notes/roofing-service-area-pages).

## Go after repeat clients

Property managers and agents book again and again. Add a short section for them: how to set up recurring turnovers, invoicing, key handover. A link or mention from a local property management company or realtor can also help your SEO.

## The Business Profile

List move-out cleaning as a service on your profile, with a short description. Add photos of finished move-out cleans: empty kitchens, clean ovens, spotless bathrooms. Ask landlords and agents for reviews after repeat jobs. Our [categories by trade list](/notes/gbp-categories-by-trade) covers the cleaning categories.

## Seasonality

Move-outs peak at the end of each month and in summer, when leases turn over and families move. Make sure the page is live and indexed well before the summer rush. Our [seasonal content calendar](/notes/seasonal-content-calendar-home-services) shows the timing by trade.
