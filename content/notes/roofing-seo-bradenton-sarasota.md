Our own Search Console shows searches like "roofing seo bradenton" and "lakewood ranch seo company" from roofers on the Gulf coast. We've done most of our roofing work on Florida's east coast, in Brevard County, so this is what we'd do here based on that work and on how this market is built.

## Three markets in one area

- **Bradenton and Palmetto.** Plenty of older homes, many shingle roofs reaching replacement age, and a lot of repair searches.
- **Sarasota, Siesta Key and the keys.** Coastal homes, tile and metal roofs, salt air, and higher-end replacements.
- **Lakewood Ranch and the planned communities.** Newer homes, strong HOAs, and questions about approved colors, materials and the approval process.

A roofer who treats all three as "Sarasota area" ends up with pages that answer none of them well.

## Pages to build

1. **Service pages.** Repair, replacement and inspections, plus a page for each roof type you install. Our [roof repair page guide](/notes/roof-repair-service-page) and [roof replacement page guide](/notes/roof-replacement-service-page) cover what goes on them.
2. **A page per town you want work in.** Bradenton, Sarasota, Lakewood Ranch, Venice, Palmetto, with jobs and reviews from each. See [service area pages](/notes/roofing-service-area-pages).
3. **An HOA page for the planned communities.** How approvals work, what information you prepare for the HOA, typical timelines. Point to the community's own rules rather than quoting them.
4. **Storm pages**, written before June. See [storm damage pages](/notes/storm-damage-roofing-pages).

## The map pack

The map pack depends a lot on where the searcher is. A roofer based in Bradenton will find it hard to show up in the map for someone searching in Venice. That's normal. The regular results below the map lean more on relevance, which is where your town pages compete.

Get the basics right on the profile: roofing contractor as primary category, the services you offer, real photos and steady reviews that mention the town and the job. [Business Profile for roofers](/notes/google-business-profile-for-roofers) has the checklist.

## Reviews that name the place

"Replaced our roof in Lakewood Ranch, handled the HOA approval for us" is a review that helps you in two ways: it shows up for local searches, and it answers the question the next HOA homeowner has. Ask for reviews that mention the town and what you did.

## Track before you scale

Before adding more towns, make sure you can see which pages bring calls. [Call tracking for contractors](/notes/call-tracking-for-contractors) shows the free setup.
