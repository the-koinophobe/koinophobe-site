Carpet cleaning is a price-compared, results-driven purchase. People want to know what it costs for their rooms, whether the stain will come out, and whether the company is trustworthy enough to let in. The site that answers those three things clearly gets the booking.

## Pages by service

People search for each of these separately:

1. **Carpet cleaning**, with your method explained in plain words
2. **Pet stain and odor removal**, a very common search with its own worries
3. **Upholstery cleaning**: sofas, chairs, mattresses
4. **Tile and grout cleaning**
5. **Area rug cleaning**, on site or picked up
6. **Commercial carpet cleaning**, if you do it

Each page: what's included, what results to expect, drying time, and price.

## Be clear on price

"How much does carpet cleaning cost" is one of the trade's biggest searches. Publish price per room or package prices, and define what a room is, what's extra (stairs, hallways, heavy stains, moving furniture) and any minimum charge. Clear prices bring fewer surprises on the day and fewer bad reviews.

## Answer the stain questions

People search for specific problems: pet urine, red wine, coffee, old stains. A page or section on stains you handle, with honest expectations about what can and can't come out, ranks for these searches and builds trust. Promising every stain will vanish does the opposite.

## Business Profile

- **Primary category:** Carpet cleaning service
- **Secondary categories** for upholstery or tile cleaning if you do them. See [categories by trade](/notes/gbp-categories-by-trade).
- **Photos:** before and after, your van, your equipment, your technicians
- **Reviews:** ask right after the job, when the carpet looks its best and the customer can see the difference

## Town pages

Carpet cleaning is local and route-based. Pages for the towns you serve, with jobs and reviews from each, help you show up beyond your immediate area. See [service area pages](/notes/roofing-service-area-pages).

## Turn customers into repeat bookings

Most homes need carpet cleaning once or twice a year. Keep a list of past customers and send a reminder when it's time. It's not SEO, but it doubles the value of every customer SEO brings you. Ask returning customers for a review too; repeat customers write the most convincing ones.

## Track bookings

Count calls, quote requests and online bookings in GA4, and note which pages they came from. [Form tracking in GA4](/notes/ga4-form-tracking-contractors) covers the setup.

## Next step

Search "carpet cleaning [your town]" and compare your prices page with the top three. If yours doesn't show prices, or doesn't have a separate pet stain page, start there. For the wider cleaning setup, see [cleaning company SEO](/notes/cleaning-company-seo).
