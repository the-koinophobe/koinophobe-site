Most contractors know their marketing spend to the dollar and have no idea how many calls they missed last month. That second number is often where the easiest money is.

## Why it matters

Someone searching "roof leak repair" or "AC not working" usually calls a few companies. If you don't pick up, the next one on the list probably will. Getting a lead to call costs you SEO work, ad spend or both. An unanswered call loses all of it.

## Step 1: get the number

Where to look:

- **Your phone provider.** Most business phone systems show missed and unanswered calls in an online dashboard.
- **Call tracking software**, if you use it. It logs whether each call was answered, by source. See [when call tracking software is worth it](/notes/call-tracking-software-contractors).
- **Your mobile phone's call log**, at minimum, if the business line rings to a cell phone.

Pull a full month. Count total calls, answered calls and missed calls.

## Step 2: find the pattern

Sort the missed calls by:

- **Time of day.** Lunch, early morning and after 5 p.m. are common gaps.
- **Day of week.** Saturdays often go unanswered while searches keep coming.
- **Season.** Storm weeks and heat waves bring more calls than one person can take.
- **Source**, if you track it. Missed calls from your Business Profile or a service page are leads you were already winning in search.

The pattern tells you what to fix.

## Step 3: fix the biggest gap first

- **Missed-call text back.** Many phone systems can send an automatic text: "Sorry we missed you, we'll call back within 15 minutes. What do you need help with?" It keeps the lead from moving on.
- **A callback routine.** Someone checks missed calls every hour during business hours and calls back.
- **Cover the busy hours.** If most misses happen at lunch, stagger breaks or forward to a second phone.
- **An answering service** for nights and weekends, if your trade gets urgent calls then.
- **Honest hours on your profile.** If you don't answer Saturdays, don't list Saturday hours.

## Step 4: track it monthly

Add one line to your monthly numbers: missed calls as a share of all calls. If it falls, you're getting more from the same marketing. Our [one-page report template](/notes/monthly-seo-report-template) has room for it.

## Before you spend more on SEO

If a big share of calls go unanswered, more traffic means more missed calls. Fixing the phone is usually cheaper than buying more leads, and it makes every future dollar of SEO work harder.
