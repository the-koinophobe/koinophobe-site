When you set up a service area business on Google, you get a box that accepts up to 20 areas. The natural move is to fill it. That usually does less than owners expect, and sometimes it hurts.

## What the service area does

Your service area tells Google, and customers, where you work. It's shown on your profile and it helps Google understand which searches are relevant to you. If you don't have a storefront, it replaces the address on your public profile. Our [service area business guide](/notes/service-area-business-google-business-profile) covers the setup.

What it doesn't do is override distance. The map pack still leans heavily on how far the searcher is from your verified location. A plumber in Palm Bay with Titusville in the service area still rarely shows in the map for a search made in Titusville.

## Google's limits

- **Up to 20 service areas** per profile.
- **About two hours' drive** from your base, per Google's guidelines. Areas further away aren't supposed to be listed.

## How many we'd actually add

For most contractors: **the towns you work in every month**, usually five to twelve. That keeps the profile honest and matches what customers see when they check whether you cover their town.

Leave out:

- Towns you'd only drive to for a large job
- Whole states or huge regions
- Places you added just because a competitor is based there

## What actually wins farther towns

If you want work in a town 40 minutes away, the service area box won't win it on its own. What helps:

1. **A town page on your site** with jobs, photos and reviews from there. The regular results below the map rely less on distance than the map does. See [service area pages](/notes/roofing-service-area-pages).
2. **Reviews that mention the town.** They help relevance for searches there.
3. **Links and mentions from that town**: a local supplier, a chamber of commerce, a community sponsor.

## Should I open another location?

Only if it's real: an office or shop with staff, where you can receive mail and customers. Virtual offices and mailbox addresses break Google's guidelines and are a common cause of suspensions. If it happens, see [suspended profiles](/notes/google-business-profile-suspended).

## Keep it consistent

The towns in your service area should match the towns you list on your site and on your town pages. Saying the same thing everywhere makes it easier for Google, and for AI assistants, to trust where you work.

## Next step

Open your profile's service area and remove any town you haven't worked in during the last year. Then check that each remaining town has a page or at least a mention on your site. Our [map pack ranking guide](/notes/google-map-pack-ranking) covers what moves you up once the area is right.
