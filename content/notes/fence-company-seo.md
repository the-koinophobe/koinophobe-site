People buying a fence know roughly what they want: privacy, a safe yard for the dog, a pool barrier, or just something that looks better than the old one. They search by material and by purpose, and almost all of them have the same questions about permits, property lines and how long it takes.

Answer those well and you win the call.

## Pages by material

Each material is a separate search with its own questions:

1. **Vinyl fencing**: durability, colors, maintenance
2. **Wood fencing**: styles, staining, lifespan
3. **Aluminum and ornamental**: pool code, looks, rust
4. **Chain link**: cost, commercial use, privacy slats
5. **Composite or specialty** materials you install

Each page: what it's best for, typical lifespan, maintenance, and what changes the price.

## Pages by purpose

People also search by why they want a fence:

- **Privacy fence**
- **Pool fence**, where local safety rules apply
- **Dog fence** and yard enclosures
- **Commercial and security fencing**, if you do it

These pages can link to the matching materials.

## Answer the permit question

"Do I need a permit for a fence" is one of the most common searches in the trade. Write a page that explains how it usually works in your area: whether permits are needed, height limits, HOA approval, and setbacks. Then link to each town's building department for the official rules, which change. Mention whether you handle permits for the customer.

Property lines come up too: explain why a survey matters and who's responsible for locating lines.

## Town pages

For each town you serve, a page with fences you've built there, the materials common in that area, and any local quirks such as HOA rules in planned communities. [Service area pages](/notes/roofing-service-area-pages) explains how to make them more than a template.

## Photos that work

Real fences in real yards, each captioned: "6-foot white vinyl privacy fence in Palm Bay." These captions help the gallery show up for specific searches. See [before and after galleries](/notes/before-after-gallery-seo).

## Business Profile

- **Primary category:** Fence contractor
- **Services:** each material and type listed separately
- **Photos:** added monthly from finished jobs
- **Reviews:** ask after the final walkthrough, and encourage mentions of the material and the crew

## Seasons

Fence demand rises in spring and summer. Publish and refresh pages over the winter so they're ranking when people start planning. Our [seasonal content calendar](/notes/seasonal-content-calendar-home-services) covers timing by trade.
