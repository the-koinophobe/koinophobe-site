"Roofing SEO expert" is a title anyone can use. The best way to judge one is to see what they find and do in the first 30 days. A real expert's first month is specific to your site, your towns and your profile. A weak one sends a generic report and a promise.

Here's what the first month should produce.

## Week 1: access and tracking

- **Access to your accounts**, in your name: Google Analytics, Search Console, Business Profile, website. If they want to own them, that's a red flag.
- **Call and form tracking set up and tested**, so every later decision has numbers behind it. See [call tracking for contractors](/notes/call-tracking-for-contractors).
- **A baseline**: last month's and last year's calls, clicks, profile actions and positions for your main searches.

## Week 2: what's broken

A real audit finds problems specific to your site:

- Pages Google can't or won't index. See [why a contractor site isn't showing on Google](/notes/contractor-website-not-showing-on-google).
- Forms or phone links that don't work on mobile
- Slow pages, especially on phones
- Duplicate town pages Google is ignoring. See [town pages and canonical errors](/notes/city-pages-canonical).
- Business Profile problems: wrong primary category, missing services, old photos, hours that don't match the site

## Week 3: the fixes that cost calls

The most damaging problems fixed first: a broken form, a page blocked from Google, a wrong category. These fixes are often where the first quick gains come from.

## Week 4: the plan

A ranked plan for the next three to six months:

- Which service pages to build or rewrite. Our [roof repair](/notes/roof-repair-service-page) and [roof replacement](/notes/roof-replacement-service-page) guides show what good ones contain.
- Which towns to build pages for, and why those first
- Profile and review work
- How progress will be reported, ideally on [one page](/notes/monthly-seo-report-template)

## Questions to ask before you sign

1. Can you show Search Console data from roofing sites you've worked on?
2. Will tracking be set up in my accounts?
3. What will I be able to see that you did each month?
4. What happens to the accounts and pages if I leave?

More in [questions to ask before hiring an SEO](/notes/hiring-an-seo-questions-to-ask).
