Nobody searches for water damage restoration on a calm afternoon. They search with water on the floor, often at night, and they call the first company that looks real. Lead-generation sites know this too, so restoration is one of the most crowded local searches there is.

Here's how a local company wins it.

## The Business Profile decides the first call

For "water damage restoration near me," the map pack sits at the top. To win it:

- **Hours that are true.** If someone answers at 2 a.m., say 24 hours. If not, don't. Mismatched hours bring bad reviews fast.
- **The right primary category**, such as water damage restoration service, with secondaries only for work you do: mold remediation, fire damage restoration.
- **Real photos.** Your trucks, equipment and crews on site, not stock images.
- **Reviews that describe the emergency.** "They were at our house in under an hour after our water heater burst" is the review the next customer needs.

Fake addresses are common in this industry. Don't join in: Google suspends them, and you can [report the ones near you](/notes/report-fake-google-business-listings).

## A page for every kind of damage

Homeowners search by what happened:

1. Burst pipe and plumbing leaks
2. Flooded basement
3. Water heater leaks
4. Storm and roof leaks
5. Sewage backup
6. Mold after water damage

Each page: what to do right now (shut off the water, stay safe around electricity), how fast you respond, what the process looks like, and how insurance usually works.

## Speed on the page, too

Most of these searches happen on phones, under stress. The page should load fast and show the phone number without scrolling. A slow page loses the call to whoever's next. Our [Core Web Vitals guide](/notes/core-web-vitals-contractor-websites) covers the speed fixes.

## Explain insurance honestly

Most restoration jobs involve a claim. Write a page on how it works with you: documenting damage, moisture readings, photos, working with the adjuster, direct billing if you offer it. Don't promise outcomes. Explain the process clearly and homeowners will trust you with the rest.

## Town pages

List the towns you can reach quickly, with a page for each one where you've done real jobs. Mention response times honestly. [Service area pages](/notes/roofing-service-area-pages) shows how to make them more than a template.

## Count every call

Restoration leads are valuable, so missing one is expensive. Track calls by source and set up missed-call alerts. Our [call tracking software guide](/notes/call-tracking-software-contractors) explains when paid tracking earns its cost.

## Next step

Search "water damage restoration [your town]" on your phone at night, if you can. Look at what the top results promise and whether they're real local companies.
