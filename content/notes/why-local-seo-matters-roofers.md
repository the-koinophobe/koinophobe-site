Most trades have regular customers. A plumber gets called back, a lawn company mows the same yards every week, a pool company has its route. Roofing is different, and those differences are why local search matters so much for roofers.

## Roofing jobs are rare for each customer

A homeowner might replace a roof once or twice in the time they own a house. Most don't have a roofer they already trust. When they need one, they search.

That means every new job has to be won again, and search is where most of them start.

## The jobs are worth a lot

A roof replacement is one of the biggest home expenses there is. One extra job a month from search can cover a year of SEO. That's why the comparison between local SEO and [buying shared leads](/notes/shared-leads-vs-your-own-website) usually favors owning your search presence over time.

## Searches happen under pressure

A lot of roofing searches happen after something has gone wrong: a leak, missing shingles, a storm. The homeowner wants someone nearby and reliable, quickly. They mostly pick from the map pack and the first few results. If you're not there, you're not in the running.

## It's local by nature

Roofers work within a drive of their base. Google's map results lean heavily on distance, and the regular results reward pages about specific towns. Both favor roofers who build a strong local presence over companies trying to cover a whole state. Our [Florida roofing note](/notes/roofing-seo-florida) shows what that looks like in a storm-heavy market.

## Trust is the product

Homeowners worry about storm chasers, insurance scams and poor workmanship. Reviews, real photos, a real address and pages about local jobs answer those worries before the call. Local SEO and trust are mostly the same work.

## What to put first

1. **Tracking**, so you can see which searches bring calls. See [call tracking for contractors](/notes/call-tracking-for-contractors).
2. **The Business Profile**: category, services, photos and a review habit. See [Business Profile for roofers](/notes/google-business-profile-for-roofers).
3. **Service pages** for repair and replacement. See the [roof repair](/notes/roof-repair-service-page) and [roof replacement](/notes/roof-replacement-service-page) guides.
4. **Town pages** for the towns you want work in. See [service area pages](/notes/roofing-service-area-pages).
5. **Storm pages** written before storm season. See [storm damage pages](/notes/storm-damage-roofing-pages).

## What it can do

On one Brevard County roofing site, average Google position went from 47.9 to 14.9 year on year with this kind of steady local work. The numbers are on the [work page](/work).
