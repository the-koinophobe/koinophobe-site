A roof repair search usually starts with a stain on the ceiling or a few shingles in the yard. The homeowner wants someone reliable, nearby, soon. Your repair page has about ten seconds to show you're that company.

We built a [roof replacement page guide](/notes/roof-replacement-service-page) last year. Repair needs a different page, because the person reading it is in a different situation.

## Above the fold: the three things they look for

1. **That you fix their problem.** "Roof repair in [town]" in the heading, with a line listing the common repairs: leaks, missing shingles, flashing, soft spots.
2. **How fast you come out.** Your real response time. "Same-day inspections most weekdays" is worth more than any slogan.
3. **A phone number they can tap.** Tracked, so you know the page brings calls. See [call tracking for contractors](/notes/call-tracking-for-contractors).

## Symptoms, in their words

Homeowners don't search "flashing failure." They search "water stain on ceiling," "roof leaking around chimney," "shingles blew off." Add a short section for the problems you fix most, each with:

- What it usually means
- Whether it's urgent
- What a repair involves

These sections help the page show up for problem searches, and they show the homeowner you understand what they're dealing with.

## Repair or replace: answer it honestly

Every repair call has the question underneath: "Do I need a new roof?" Explain how you decide: the age of the roof, how widespread the damage is, whether repairs have been done before. Saying you'll tell them honestly when a repair is enough builds more trust than any badge.

Link to your replacement page for those who need it.

## Show real repairs

Photos of actual repairs, each with a short caption: the town, the problem, what you did. A gallery of finished roofs from a stock site does nothing. More on making photos count in [before and after galleries](/notes/before-after-gallery-seo).

## Make it local

A short section on the towns you serve, linking to your [town pages](/notes/roofing-service-area-pages). If you're in hurricane country, mention storm repairs and tarping, and link to your [storm damage page](/notes/storm-damage-roofing-pages).

## Insurance, briefly

If you help with insurance claims, say what that help looks like: documenting damage, meeting the adjuster, providing an estimate. Don't promise what the insurer will pay.

## Schema and the basics

- A title like "Roof Repair in [Town] | [Business Name]"
- RoofingContractor schema on the site, with this page's service described. Our [roofing schema guide](/notes/roofing-contractor-schema) has the markup.
- A short FAQ at the bottom with the questions you get on the phone

Read your current repair page on your phone as if your ceiling were leaking. Can you find the phone number, the response time and proof you've fixed this before, without scrolling?
