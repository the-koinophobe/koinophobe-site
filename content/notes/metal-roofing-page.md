Homeowners who search for metal roofing are usually well into their research. They've heard it lasts longer, costs more and might be noisy. They want those questions answered honestly before they call. Most roofing sites mention metal in one line; a page that answers it properly can win these jobs.

## Lead with the types you install

- **Standing seam**
- **Exposed-fastener panels**, such as corrugated or R-panel
- **Metal shingles and stone-coated steel**, if you install them

For each: what it looks like, where it suits, and roughly how it compares on cost.

## Answer the five questions everyone asks

1. **How much does it cost?** A range compared with shingle, and what moves the price: roof size, pitch, panel type, tear-off. Point to a quote for the real number.
2. **Is it noisy in the rain?** Explain how underlayment and decking affect noise, honestly.
3. **Does it make the house hot?** Cover reflective finishes and how metal handles heat.
4. **How long does it last?** Typical lifespans and what the manufacturer warranty covers.
5. **Is it good in storms?** Wind ratings and what they mean, without overselling. In hurricane country, explain the general picture and point to the local building department for code details.

These answers help the page appear for the questions people actually search.

## Colors and finishes

Show the colors and finishes you offer, with real photos, not just swatches. If you work in communities with HOA color rules, say you can help with approvals.

## Photos of local installs

A gallery of metal roofs you've installed, captioned with the town, the panel type and the color. See [before and after galleries](/notes/before-after-gallery-seo).

## Warranties and maintenance

Explain the difference between the manufacturer's warranty and your workmanship warranty, and what maintenance metal needs. Clear answers here build trust.

## Insurance, carefully

Some insurers treat certain roof types differently. Say that, suggest homeowners ask their insurer, and don't promise discounts.

Link from your [roof replacement page](/notes/roof-replacement-service-page) and from town pages where you've installed metal. Add the service to your Business Profile with a short description. Our [roofing schema guide](/notes/roofing-contractor-schema) covers marking up your services.
