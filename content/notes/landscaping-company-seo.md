Landscaping companies usually sell two very different things: one-off projects, such as a new patio or a full front-yard redesign, and ongoing maintenance. Homeowners search for them differently, research them differently and buy them at different times. A website that treats them as one thing ranks well for neither.

## Split projects from maintenance

**Projects** are researched for weeks. People search for ideas, costs and examples: "backyard patio ideas," "paver patio cost," "landscape design [town]."

**Maintenance** is a quick decision about reliability and price: "landscape maintenance near me," "yard cleanup service."

Build pages for each side:

- **Design and installation**: your process, from consultation to install
- **Hardscaping**: patios, walkways, retaining walls, fire pits, each with a page if you do a lot of it
- **Plantings and sod**
- **Irrigation**, if you install or repair it
- **Maintenance**: what's included, schedules, pricing approach
- **Seasonal cleanups**

## The gallery does the selling

For project work, the gallery is the most important page on the site. For each project:

- Before and after photos
- The town and neighborhood
- What was done, with materials and plants named
- A rough timeline, and the budget range if the client is happy to share it

Named materials and plants help these pages show up for specific searches. Our [before and after gallery guide](/notes/before-after-gallery-seo) explains the setup.

## Business Profile

- **Primary category:** Landscaper, or Landscape designer if design is most of the work
- **Secondary categories** only for work you do: lawn care service, irrigation, hardscaping-related categories. See [categories by trade](/notes/gbp-categories-by-trade).
- **Photos** every month from current projects
- **Reviews** that describe the project, the crew and the result

## Answer cost questions

"How much does landscaping cost" is too broad to answer, but "how much does a paver patio cost in [area]" isn't. Pages with honest ranges from your own projects, and what moves the price, rank for these and bring better-informed leads.

## Town pages

Landscaping varies by area: lot sizes, soil, HOA rules, sun exposure. Pages for the towns you work in, with projects from each, do better than one general page. See [service area pages](/notes/roofing-service-area-pages).

## Time it to the season

Project research starts in late winter for spring installs. Publish and refresh project pages then. Maintenance sign-ups peak in early spring. Our [seasonal content calendar](/notes/seasonal-content-calendar-home-services) has the timing, and if mowing is part of your business, see [lawn mowing SEO](/notes/lawn-mowing-seo).

## Next step

Pick your five best projects from last year and write each up as a gallery entry with town, materials and plants. Then check you have separate pages for projects and maintenance.
