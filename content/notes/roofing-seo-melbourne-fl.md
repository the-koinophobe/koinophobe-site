Melbourne is a hard place to rank a roofer. It's in Brevard County, where hurricanes and insurance keep demand high, so plenty of local companies compete for it, and lead sites and out-of-area crews pile in after every storm.

We've done SEO for a roofing contractor here. Its Search Console data is on our [work page](/work). Here's what changed and what we'd do again.

## The numbers

Comparing October 2025 to August 2026 with the eleven months before:

- **Average Google position across the site:** 47.9 to 14.9
- **"roofing melbourne fl":** about position 77 to 12.9
- **"roof replacement melbourne fl":** about 70 to 15.0
- **"roof repair melbourne fl":** about 51 to 18.0

Page eight to page two, roughly. Not page one yet, which is where the next round of work goes.

## What we did

**1. Fixed what Google couldn't read.** Indexing problems, slow mobile pages and a contact form that wasn't reliably delivering. Nothing else matters until those are fixed. Our [contractor site checklist](/notes/contractor-website-not-showing-on-google) covers the usual suspects.

**2. Built a page for each service.** Roof repair, roof replacement, and each roof type the company installs, each with its own photos and its own questions. A single "Roofing services" page can't rank for all of those searches.

**3. Built town pages with real jobs.** Melbourne, Palm Bay, Viera, West Melbourne and the beaches, each with jobs done there. More on that in [roofing service area pages](/notes/roofing-service-area-pages).

**4. Cleaned up the Business Profile.** The right primary category, services listed, recent photos, and a steady review habit. See [Business Profile for roofers](/notes/google-business-profile-for-roofers).

**5. Tracked calls.** So the monthly report could say whether more people called, not just whether rankings moved.

## What we'd do next

Page two is close but not where the calls are. The next round for a Melbourne roofer:

- **Storm pages written ahead of the season.** So they're indexed before the next storm, not written during it. See [storm damage roofing pages](/notes/storm-damage-roofing-pages).
- **More reviews that name the town.** "New roof in Suntree, done in two days" helps more than a star rating alone.
- **Titles that earn the click.** Some pages show up often and get few clicks. Rewriting titles is one of the cheapest wins; we explain why in [zero-click rankings](/notes/zero-click-rankings-title-tags).

## Melbourne-specific searches worth a page

From what homeowners search here:

- Roof repair and replacement by neighborhood: Suntree, Viera, Eau Gallie, the beachside
- Roof inspections for insurance and home sales
- Tile and metal roofing, both common in the area
- Emergency tarping after storms

Each is a page if you do the work.
