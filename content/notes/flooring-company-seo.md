Flooring is a big, visible decision that homeowners live with for years. They compare materials, look at samples, worry about pets and kids and spills, and get more than one quote. The flooring company whose site answers those comparisons clearly gets the showroom visit or the in-home measure.

## Pages by material

Each material is its own search, with its own buyers:

1. **Luxury vinyl plank (LVP)**, often the most searched
2. **Hardwood**: solid and engineered
3. **Laminate**
4. **Tile**: porcelain, ceramic, stone
5. **Carpet**
6. **Refinishing**, if you refinish existing hardwood

For each: pros and cons, durability with pets and kids, water resistance, maintenance, and an installed price range.

## Comparisons people search

"LVP vs laminate," "engineered vs solid hardwood," "best flooring for dogs." Honest comparison pages rank for these and position you as the expert. Don't pretend one material suits everyone.

## Prices people can use

Give installed price ranges per square foot from your own work, and what changes them: removing old floors, subfloor repairs, stairs, transitions, furniture moving. Explain that measuring sets the final quote.

## Showroom and in-home quotes

If you have a showroom, give it a page: hours, what's on display, whether appointments are needed, and directions. If you bring samples to homes, explain how in-home quotes work. Either way, make booking easy.

## Real installs

A gallery of floors you've installed, captioned with the town, room, material and color. Before and after photos are especially convincing for flooring. See [before and after galleries](/notes/before-after-gallery-seo).

## Business Profile

- **Primary category** matching your main business, such as a flooring contractor or flooring store category, with others only for real work
- **Photos** of installs and of the showroom
- **Reviews** that mention the material, the installers and the result

Only create a profile where customers can actually visit. See [service area businesses](/notes/service-area-business-google-business-profile) if you don't have a showroom.

## Town pages

Pages for the towns you install in, with jobs from each. See [service area pages](/notes/roofing-service-area-pages).

## Track visits and quotes

Count showroom appointments, quote requests and calls by page, so you know which material pages bring business. Our [lead tracking sheet](/notes/lead-tracking-sheet) shows how to connect leads to booked jobs.
