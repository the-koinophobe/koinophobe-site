Every owner asks this on the first call, and I won't give a date. Google decides when it recrawls your pages and how it ranks them, and anyone who promises page one by a certain month is guessing.

What I can do is show you how it went on real sites, month by month, and what happens in each stretch.

## A clinic, month by month

This is a pain clinic in Florida that I work on under an agency's name. Here are its clicks from Google search, straight from Search Console.

| Month | Clicks |
| --- | --- |
| August 2025 | 2 |
| September 2025 | 4 |
| October 2025 | 49 |
| November 2025 | 59 |
| December 2025 | 60 |
| January 2026 | 74 |
| February 2026 | 62 |
| March 2026 | 72 |
| April 2026 | 104 |
| May 2026 | 96 |
| June 2026 | 94 |
| July 2026 | 95 |
| August 2026 (partial) | 83 |

That's 854 clicks over the whole stretch. A few things to notice. The first two months were close to nothing. The jump came in the third month. After that it climbed slowly, dipped in February, and has sat between 83 and 104 a month since April. 678 of those 854 clicks came from phones.

The shape is the useful part. Small for a while, a step up, then a slower climb with dips along the way.

## A roofing site, over a year

The second example moved slower, because roofing is a more crowded search. On a Florida roofing site I work on, I compared October 2025 to August 2026 against the eleven months before.

![A calendar with red push pins marking dates](/notes/photos/how-long-does-seo-take-1.webp "Photo: Towfiqu barbhuiya on Unsplash")

Average position went from 47.9 to 14.9. Impressions went from 5,410 to 139,639. Clicks went from 34 to 266. 893 of the 1,099 searches it now shows for didn't show it at all the year before.

Its click-through rate fell from 0.63% to 0.19% over the same period, because most of the new impressions were on page two. That's normal in the middle of a climb. A site shows up on page two for a search long before it reaches page one, and impressions down there rarely turn into clicks. The full case is on my [roofing SEO page](/roofing-seo).

## What happens in month 1, 3, 6 and 12

This is how the work usually runs. The numbers in each stretch differ from site to site.

**Month 1.** Setup and fixes. Call and form tracking in GA4, the Business Profile cleaned up, indexing problems found and fixed, speed and schema work. Rankings usually barely move, because Google hasn't recrawled most of the changes yet.

**Month 3.** New and rewritten pages are indexed. In Search Console you start to see more impressions and more searches the site shows for, often on page two or three. On the clinic, this is the month clicks jumped.

**Month 6.** Some of those searches have reached page one. You can compare the last three months against the three before and see whether the line is going up.

**Month 12.** Now you can compare against the same months last year, which takes seasons out of the picture. For a roofer, hurricane season (June 1 to November 30 in the Atlantic) can swing the numbers a lot, so a year-over-year comparison is the honest one.

If you want to read these numbers yourself, [Google Search Console for contractors](/notes/google-search-console-for-contractors) covers the Performance report and the date comparison I use.

## Tracking shows wins before rankings do

Rankings take months. Tracking can show you something in the first few weeks.

Once phone taps and form submissions are tracked, you know how many leads the site already brings in, which pages they come from, and which source sent them. Sometimes the first win is a fix. In July 2026 I found that a client's form submissions had been dropping since March because of an email setting that broke after an email address change. Four months, and nobody noticed, me included. I test form delivery on every site monthly now.

That's why tracking is the first thing I set up. I wrote about how in [call tracking for contractors](/notes/call-tracking-for-contractors), and the map side of the timeline is in [how to rank in the Google map pack](/notes/google-map-pack-ranking).

## Why my plans have a 3-month minimum

Month one is setup. Google needs time after that to recrawl and re-rank what changed. If you judge the work at week six, you're mostly judging the setup. A 3-month minimum gives the changes time to show up in Search Console, then the plan runs month to month with 30 days' notice. You own every account the whole time.
