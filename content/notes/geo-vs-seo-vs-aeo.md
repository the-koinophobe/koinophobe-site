Every few months the industry invents a new acronym for helping customers find you. Right now there are at least five: SEO, AEO, GEO, AI SEO and LLM SEO. If you run a business, the useful question isn't which name is right. It's whether anything new is being sold, and what it's worth.

Here's how I see them, with the overlap measured where I can.

## The short definitions

| Term | Stands for | Aims to | Where it shows |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SEO | Search engine optimization | Rank your pages | Google and Bing results, maps |
| AEO | Answer engine optimization | Be the answer that gets lifted | Featured snippets, voice, AI answers |
| GEO | Generative engine optimization | Be named and cited in AI-written answers | ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, AI Overviews |
| AI SEO, LLM SEO | Same idea as GEO | Same as GEO | Same as GEO |

AEO is the oldest of the new ones. It grew out of featured snippets and voice search: write a direct answer high on the page so a machine can lift it. GEO came from a 2023 research paper and covers the newer case, where an AI writes a whole answer and cites a few sources. I wrote a fuller explainer of [generative engine optimization](/notes/generative-engine-optimization).

## How much they overlap

Mostly. Three things from [my September 2026 citation study](/notes/ai-cited-vs-ranked-page) show it:

- **Companies overlap a lot.** 34% to 54% of the companies cited by ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews also ranked in Google's top ten for the same question. Ranking is a big part of being named.
- **Pages overlap a little.** Only 2% to 12% of cited pages were in that top ten. Which of your pages gets quoted is the part SEO alone doesn't settle.
- **The basics are shared.** Every assistant I measured needs to fetch your page to cite it. Crawl access, readable text, fast pages and clear writing help all five acronyms at once.

## What's new with GEO

Two things, and they're worth paying attention to:

**1. AI crawler access.** SEO has always checked Googlebot. GEO also has to check OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot and PerplexityBot, and the firewall settings that block them. Plenty of sites that rank fine on Google are invisible to ChatGPT because of one robots.txt line. [How to check yours](/notes/ai-crawlers-robots-txt).

**2. Measuring the answers.** Google gives you Search Console. AI assistants mostly don't give you anything comparable yet (Bing's new [AI Performance report](/notes/bing-copilot-ai-performance-report) is the first real exception). So GEO has to measure answers directly, and because answers move a lot between runs, it has to repeat them. In my study, ChatGPT kept only 4.5% of its cited pages across three runs of the same question. [How to track it](/notes/track-ai-search-visibility).

Everything else sold as GEO, such as better content, structured data, digital PR and reviews, is SEO work with a new label. That doesn't make it worthless. It means you shouldn't pay twice for it.

## How to tell new from renamed when you're buying

Ask a provider three questions:

1. **"How will you measure whether we're showing up in AI answers?"** A good answer involves a fixed list of questions, several assistants and repeated runs. A weak answer is screenshots.
2. **"What will you do that our SEO work doesn't already cover?"** A good answer names crawler access and answer tracking. A weak answer is "optimize for AI."
3. **"Do you guarantee placement in ChatGPT?"** The right answer is no.
