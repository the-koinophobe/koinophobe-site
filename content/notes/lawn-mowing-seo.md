Weekly mowing is the steadiest work in lawn care. A customer who signs up in March can stay for years. Most of them choose in a few weeks every spring, after searching "lawn mowing service near me" and calling the first two or three companies that look reliable.

The work to win them happens in winter.

## Mowing deserves its own page

Many lawn care sites put mowing in one line on a services page next to fertilization and cleanups. Mowing is searched on its own, by people with a simple question: who can cut my grass every week, and for how much?

A mowing page should say:

- **What's included**: mowing, edging, trimming, blowing off hard surfaces
- **Frequency**: weekly, biweekly, and how you handle the slow season
- **Price**: a starting price or range by lot size. If you can't publish numbers, say what changes it: lot size, slopes, gates, frequency.
- **How scheduling works**: same day each week, what happens when it rains
- **How to start**: a short quote form or a number to call

Our existing [lawn care SEO note](/notes/lawn-care-seo) covers the full service range; this page is the one that wins mowing.

## Route-based neighborhood pages

Mowing companies work in routes, and route density is profit. Build pages for the neighborhoods and towns where you already have customers and want more. On each:

- Mention that you already mow on certain streets or in certain neighborhoods, if your customers agree
- Typical lot sizes and grass types there
- A review from a customer in that area

These pages rank for "lawn mowing [town]" and pull customers into routes you already drive. See [service area pages](/notes/roofing-service-area-pages) for how to make them worth indexing.

## Business Profile

- **Primary category:** Lawn care service
- **Services:** mowing, edging, cleanups, fertilization and anything else you do, listed separately
- **Photos:** striped lawns, clean edges, your trucks and trailers in real neighborhoods
- **Reviews:** ask in the first month, once the customer has seen consistent work. Reliability is what mowing reviews should mention.

## Answer the questions people search

- How much does lawn mowing cost?
- How often should grass be cut?
- Do I need to be home?
- What happens when it rains?

Each can be a short section on the mowing page or a short page of its own.

## Time it to the season

Publish or refresh the mowing page and neighborhood pages in winter, so they're indexed and climbing by early spring. Our [seasonal content calendar](/notes/seasonal-content-calendar-home-services) shows the timing.

## Track sign-ups

Count calls and quote requests in GA4, by page. You'll see which neighborhoods respond and where to add the next page. [Form tracking in GA4](/notes/ga4-form-tracking-contractors) covers the setup.

## Next step

Search "lawn mowing [your town]" and see whether a mowing-specific page of yours shows up. If you only have a general services page, build the mowing page this month, before the spring rush.
