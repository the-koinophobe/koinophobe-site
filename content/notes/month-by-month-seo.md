"SEO takes time" is true, and it's also what people say when nothing is happening. Here's what should be done, and what should start to change, in each of the first six months for a home service business. Use it to hold your agency to account, or to plan the work yourself.

For how long results usually take, read [how long SEO takes](/notes/how-long-does-seo-take). This note is the month-by-month version.

## Month 1: measure and fix

**Done:**
- Call and form tracking set up and tested. See [call tracking for contractors](/notes/call-tracking-for-contractors).
- Search Console and GA4 in your name, with access shared.
- A review of the site and Business Profile, with problems ranked by what costs calls.
- The worst problems fixed: broken forms, pages Google can't index, wrong categories.

**Changes:** You can see leads for the first time. Rankings may not move yet.

## Month 2: the Business Profile and the money pages

**Done:**
- Business Profile cleaned up: categories, services, photos, hours, a review habit started.
- Your main service pages rewritten or built, one per service.
- Titles and descriptions improved on pages that show up but get few clicks.

**Changes:** Profile actions and clicks often start moving.

## Month 3: towns

**Done:**
- Town pages for the two or three towns you most want work in, each with real jobs.
- Internal links between service pages and town pages.

**Changes:** New pages start appearing in Search Console, usually low at first.

## Month 4: depth

**Done:**
- More service or town pages, one or two a month.
- Answers to the questions customers ask on the phone, as pages or sections.
- A first round of local links: suppliers, associations, sponsorships.

**Changes:** Positions for your main searches should be climbing compared with month one.

## Month 5: what's working, more of it

**Done:**
- A look at which pages bring leads, and more pages like them.
- Fixes to pages that get visits but no calls: speed, layout, phone number, form.

**Changes:** Leads from Google should be up on the same month last year, or there should be a clear reason why not.

## Month 6: review

**Done:**
- A half-year review: leads, profile actions, clicks and positions against six months ago and the same period last year.
- A plan for the next six months based on what worked.

**Changes:** You should be able to say, with numbers, whether SEO is paying for itself or on the way to it.
