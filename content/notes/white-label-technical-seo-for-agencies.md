If you run a marketing agency with home service clients, your team may be stronger on ads or content than on the technical side, like tracking and indexing. That's the part I do, under your name.

Of the 15 sites on [my portfolio wall](/work), 11 went out under an agency's name. Much of my work is done this way.

## What I do under your brand

**Audits.** A full technical pass on a client site: indexing, titles and meta descriptions, site structure, speed, schema and tracking. You get findings written so you can hand them to your client as your own.

**Tracking.** Phone taps and form submissions as key events in GA4, set up through Google Tag Manager, with Search Console linked. I also test form delivery on every site monthly. That habit started in July 2026, when I found a client's form submissions had been dropping since March because the email connection needed re-authenticating after an email address change. Nobody had noticed, me included.

**Speed.** Core Web Vitals work against Google's "good" thresholds: LCP of 2.5 seconds or less, INP of 200 milliseconds or less, CLS of 0.1 or less. On my own site, mobile PageSpeed went from 52 to 96, then 99, after I removed heavy animation libraries and unused scripts.

**Schema.** The right business type (RoofingContractor, Plumber, HVACBusiness and so on), Service markup on service pages, and BlogPosting on articles.

**Fixes.** The ones that come up in real client work. On one site, a speed plugin's combine and minify setting stopped forms from submitting. The fix was turning that setting off, excluding the page from caching and purging all caches.

**Pages.** Town and service pages that are each written for that place, so they stay clear of Google's doorway rules. For one roofing client I built a flat roof repair page from the client's own job photos, compressed, renamed and converted to WebP, and linked it with the main services page.

## How it works

Your agency's name goes on the reports. I stay invisible to your client unless you want otherwise.

Your client still owns every account. I get added as a user to their Analytics, Search Console, Tag Manager and Business Profile, the same way your own staff would be. If the client leaves you, nothing is stuck with me.

I work 9 to 5 Eastern and reply the same business day, which matters when your client calls you about a form that stopped working.

## What the results look like

Two of my clearer results were white-label.

A Florida roofing site went from an average position of 47.9 to 14.9, comparing October 2025 to August 2026 with the eleven months before. Impressions went from 5,410 to 139,639, and clicks from 34 to 266. The work was title and meta rewrites across the site, a service page per town instead of one county page, FAQ and service schema, and long-form articles on the questions Florida homeowners ask after storms. The details are on my [roofing SEO page](/roofing-seo).

A Florida pain clinic went from 4 clicks in its first full month to 104 in April 2026, and stayed in the mid-90s through July.
