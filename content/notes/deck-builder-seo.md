A new deck is a planned purchase. Homeowners look at photos for weeks, compare wood and composite, and try to work out what it should cost before talking to anyone. The deck builder whose site helps them do that is usually the one they call.

## Pages by material

1. **Composite decking**, with the brands you install
2. **Pressure-treated wood**
3. **Cedar or hardwood**, if you build with them
4. **PVC decking**
5. **Railings**: aluminum, cable, glass, wood

For each: cost compared with the others, maintenance, lifespan, and what it suits.

## Pages by project

- New deck builds
- Deck replacement
- Deck repair and resurfacing
- Covered decks, pergolas and screened porches, if you do them
- Multi-level and second-story decks

## An honest cost page

"How much does a deck cost" is one of the trade's biggest searches. Give ranges from your own projects, per square foot or by typical size, and list what moves the price: material, height, railings, stairs, permits, site access. Explain that a site visit sets the final number. Honest ranges bring better-prepared leads.

## The gallery

Deck buyers choose with their eyes. For each project:

- Photos from several angles, ideally before and after
- Town and neighborhood
- Material, color and railing type, named
- Size and any notable features

Named materials help the gallery appear for specific searches. See [before and after galleries](/notes/before-after-gallery-seo).

## Permits

Most new decks need permits. Explain how it generally works in your area, whether you handle it, and link to the local building department for official rules. Fence companies get the same question; our [fence company note](/notes/fence-company-seo) handles it the same way.

## Business Profile

- **Primary category:** Deck builder
- **Services** listed by project type
- **Photos** of finished decks, added regularly
- **Reviews** that mention the material and the result. Ask at the final walkthrough.

## Town pages

Pages for the towns you build in, each with projects from there. See [service area pages](/notes/roofing-service-area-pages).

## Seasons

Publish and refresh material and cost pages in winter, before planning starts. Our [seasonal content calendar](/notes/seasonal-content-calendar-home-services) shows timing by trade.
