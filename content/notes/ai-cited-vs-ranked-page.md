I recently encountered [Openbenchmarks](https://openbenchmarks.com/), an independent benchmarking platform with open weights for agents to get another source of truth regarding queries and vendors. And I thought their idea fascinating, so I decided to run a simulation, to see what these AI agents actually accorded more weight to in this current time period.

**The question**

Assistants now shortlist tools on people's behalf. When one does, it cites a handful of pages, and those pages are a small fraction of what ranks for the same question. Nobody publishes what separates the cited from the merely ranked. The existing literature on the subject is advice, not measurement.
So, narrowly: in one category, over one collection window, **is there any measurable property of a page that distinguishes pages an assistant cited from pages that ranked organically for the identical question and were never cited?**
