"How much should AI SEO cost?" is a fair question with a frustrating answer: anywhere from a few hundred dollars to tens of thousands a month, for services with the same name. I can't give you a market average I'd trust, and I'd be suspicious of anyone who claims one.

What I can do is show what drives the price, what you should get for it, and exactly what's in mine.

## What drives the price

**Pages per month.** Writing a well-researched, accurate, cited-worthy article takes hours. A plan with 12 in-depth pages a month costs more than one with two, if the pages are real.

**Technical work.** Fixing crawler access, rendering, schema, internal links and speed takes developer time. Some agencies send a report and leave the fixes to you. Others ship them. The second costs more and is worth more.

**Measurement.** Tracking a fixed list of questions across four assistants with repeated runs is real work every month. A screenshot is cheap. A method is not.

**Who does it.** An agency with account managers, a strategist and writers prices differently from a specialist doing the work directly.

**Contract terms.** Long minimums lower the monthly price and raise your risk.

![Two business people signing a contract at a table](/notes/photos/ai-seo-pricing-1.webp "Photo: Vitaly Gariev on Unsplash")

## What you should get at any price

If a provider can't say yes to these, the price doesn't matter:

- **An AI crawler access check,** covering robots.txt and CDN or firewall bot settings for OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot and Googlebot. [Why that's step one](/notes/ai-crawlers-robots-txt).
- **A fixed list of buyer questions,** agreed with you, tracked across several assistants with repeated runs. In my study ChatGPT kept only 4.5% of its cited pages across three runs of the same question, so single checks are noise. [The method](/notes/track-ai-search-visibility).
- **A set number of deliverables** you can count each month.
- **A report that states its limits.** What was measured, how, and what it can't tell you.
- **No guarantees of placement.** Anyone guaranteeing ChatGPT recommendations is selling something they can't control.

## What I'd skip paying for

- **llms.txt as a line item.** Google said in 2025 that no AI system uses it. [Details](/notes/llms-txt).
- **"AI schema packages."** Google says there's no special schema for its AI features. [Schema for AI search](/notes/schema-markup-for-ai-search).
- **One-off "AI audits" with no fixes.** Unless the price is low and the findings are specific, you're paying for a list.
